# Architectural Evolution of the City Palace, Udaipur

An interactive 3D model of how the City Palace in Udaipur grew along its ridge above Lake Pichola across four phases, from 1559 to 1930.

**Live page:** https://himanshu13ps.github.io/udaipur-city-palace-evolution/

## What it shows

| Phase | Years | Museum model |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · Mewar Mature Phase | 1559–1620 | Model 1 · 16th century |
| 2 · Mewar Mughal Early Phase | 1620–1698 | Model 2 · 17th century |
| 3 · Mewar Mughal Mature Phase | 1698–1778 | Model 3 · 18th century |
| 4 · Mewar-British Phase | 1778–1930 | Model 4 · 19th–20th century |

- Step through the phases on the timeline, or press play to watch new buildings rise out of the ridge.
- Switch viewpoints: **Model** (the museum's vitrine angle), **East face** (the "changing skyline" board), **Lake front** (the painted lake elevation) and **Plan** (the site plan).
- **Mark new** tints what each phase added. Tap any labelled building for its builder, date and a short note.

## How it was made

The scene is rebuilt from photographs of the four architectural models, the phase panels, the site plan and the lake-front painting in the City Palace Museum. Massing is simplified and positions are approximate; one model unit is about 3 m.

It is a single self-contained `index.html` using [three.js](https://threejs.org/) r147 from jsDelivr. To run it locally, open `index.html` in a browser.
